art, or exact 8x enlargements. They contain colours outside the fixed palette
and partially transparent edge pixels. Generated edits may vary pixels beyond
the hair; identical poses at the pixel level are not guaranteed.

The game_ready folder name does not imply compatibility with the original
automatic sprite loader. These character sheets need atlas preparation for it.
PNG images contain no animation timing metadata.
