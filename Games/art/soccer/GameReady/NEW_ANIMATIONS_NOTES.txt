OUTSTANDING LIMITATIONS
The image tool did not honor the requested 1024 x 1024 idle export size despite
correction requests. It also redrew details within breathing pairs rather than
producing exact pixel duplicates with a one-art-pixel upper-body translation.
Hair, eyes, silhouette, colour, and pose consistency are not pixel-exact.
Character scale and dive angles are approximate. The far-post full-stretch
poses obscure the face rather than showing eyes tracking the gloves clearly.
The images retain softened/fringed edges and are not validated against a
strict pixel palette. They are drafts, not verified flicker-free game exports.
